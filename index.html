Our Story

Born from a Cookie Emergency

"It was the first week of June. I was deep in my AI PhD Doctoral Seminar — and all I could think about was cookies."

Mid-study, the craving hit hard. I went to the fridge, found some cookie dough, baked it, and ate it in under 7 minutes. Then went straight back to my research. Effective.

That moment stuck with me. I needed a cookie — urgently — and somehow managed it. And then the thought hit: why can't anyone else in the UAE do this? Why is there no frozen cookie dough to grab off a shelf and bake at home? The idea for Emergency Cookies was born right there, textbooks open, chocolate on my fingers.

So I built it. Every recipe tested from scratch in an Abu Dhabi kitchen. The branding, the website, and the design — all created with AI. Real butter, real chocolate, real love. No shortcuts. Just cookies, exactly when you need them.
