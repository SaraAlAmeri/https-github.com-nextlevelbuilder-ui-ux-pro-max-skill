Our Story

Born in an Abu Dhabi Kitchen

"Real butter. Real chocolate. Real love. No shortcuts."

Emergency Cookies started in 2024 with a simple obsession: the perfect cookie. Not the kind from a factory. The kind that fills your home with that warm, buttery smell and comes out of the oven with crispy edges and a gooey, chocolate-pooled centre.

We make every batch by hand in Abu Dhabi, freeze them at their peak, and deliver them to your door across all UAE. All you need to do is bake. 12 minutes and you're done. That's the emergency plan.
